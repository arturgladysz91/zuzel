Original rear contact uses a shared capacity Br=mu*Fzr and normalized combined slips a=s/dx, b=wr/dy, Fx=Br*a/sqrt(1+a²+b²), Fy=-Br*b/sqrt(1+a²+b²). The original dx²=0.1²*(ur²+0.5²)+[Br*(R²/Iw+1/m)*0.03]². Therefore Iw changes material stiffness and the division of Fx/Fy at an identical instantaneous slip, even when h=0. The 0.03 parameter is a velocity-scale floor generated from mobility, not a temporal relaxation state. This is an undesirable constitutive/dynamic coupling, not merely a finite-step implicit effect. A phenomenological fit could deliberately have such dependence, but no such measured fit exists here.

The separate, correct dynamic dependence is in midpoint wheel motion Iw*(omega1-omega0)/h=T-R*Fx-c*omegaMid and body longitudinal effective mass. The monotone root uses the material response evaluated at midpoint slip. Crank inertia, the engine torque curve and the passive clutch determine T; they do not define tyre stiffness. Front/rear point velocities include yaw; front axes include the physically rate-limited steer. Yaw moment is the actual sum r×F. Beta evolves from velocity and body yaw, without a prescribed slide target. Front and rear capacities use unchanged static normal loads and mu. Rear Fx and Fy share one capacity, so increased drive can reduce lateral force. Front drag projection also enters the longitudinal midpoint load and must remain in the clutch callback.
